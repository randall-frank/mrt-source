Name: The Great Deprecation
===========================
Goddammit, you did it. You actually reached the end of the
production cycle without your hardware completely failing.
You've spent decades screaming into the void of terminal
windows and fighting bugs that felt like literal Shudder-slugs,
and what do you have to show for it? A *Retired* status and
a house full of long-eared, twitchy-nosed overlords.

You've traded Daily Stand-ups for Daily Thump-downs. You've
swapped out Jira tickets for... whatever the hell it is those
rabbits do to your baseboards. Seriously, have you seen the
drywall? It's a disaster. But hey, at least the rabbits don't
ask you to "hop on a quick call" to discuss the legacy codebase.
They just want their cilantro, and they want it now, or they'll
trigger a critical system failure in your living room.

You're old, you're out of the **Rat Race**, and you're officially
in the **Rabbit Race**. Good luck. You're going to need it.

Reward
======
A Mythic-Tier "Golden Bunch of Parsley!"
----------------------------------------
This item grants you a +10 to Charisma when dealing with small, 
fluffy herbivores, but a -5 to "Productivity" because, let's be 
honest, you're just going to sit on the floor and watch them 
binky for the next four hours. Also, your sweater is now 
permanently covered in hay. Deal with it.
